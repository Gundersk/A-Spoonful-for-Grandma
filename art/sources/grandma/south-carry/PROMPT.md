# Запрос генерации

Инструмент: встроенный imagegen, transparent_background=true. Референсы: два кадра walk_S и carry_idle_S из game/images. Создан новый лист цельных фигур, исходные файлы не редактировались.

```text
Create a NEW production pixel-art sprite animation sheet for an existing top-down 2D game. References 1 and 2 are the approved grandmother walking SOUTH (toward viewer), showing the existing walking foot rhythm. Reference 3 is the exact grandmother identity, clothing, and raised-arms carry pose to match. Generate ONLY the missing SOUTH/front-view walking while carrying animation, four full-body frames arranged in a perfectly regular 2 by 2 grid on genuine transparent background. Reading order is playback order. Frame1 left leg forward/right leg behind, frame2 passing feet close beneath body, frame3 right leg forward/left behind (clearly opposite to frame1), frame4 other passing pose. Feet must visibly alternate and lift, NOT two feet moving together or merely a bouncing torso. Front-facing grandma, identical face and silver bun, burgundy floral shawl, purple skirt, cream apron with pink flowers, brown shoes; faithful matching pixel texture, palette, silhouette and chibi proportions of reference3. BOTH hands raised empty above shoulder/head sides in every frame, exactly two arms/two hands, carrying an imaginary pot above her head; no pot/tray/objects. Keep smiling face, outfit and torso very consistent between frames; movement primarily natural alternating legs, slight skirt response. Keep her full head, bun, hands and BOTH shoes completely visible with generous transparent margins in EACH cell. Exact same size and camera in all four cells, centered horizontally with consistent head anchor and foot ground line; no cuts, no shadows, no labels, no grid lines, no scenery, no duplicated ghost legs. High-quality consistent game sprites; retain original style, not realistic, not smooth vector.
```
