# Генерация переноски по верхней диагонали

Built-in imagegen, прозрачный фон. Референсы: carry_walk_NW000 (образ/руки), walk_NW000 (ракурс) и отклонённая первая генерация с недостаточным чередованием ног. Отклонённый вариант не используется в игре. Окончательный запрос:

```text
NEW full-body pixel sprite sheet, exact 2x2 grid, transparent background. Same granny as references: grey bun, burgundy floral shawl, purple dress, cream apron tied behind, brown boots, both EMPTY arms raised to carry something overhead. She faces NORTHWEST in ALL frames: back-left three-quarter view with left ear and a tiny left cheek visible, moving away upper-left. Reference1 is the identity and raised arms; reference2 shows her normal northwest walking. Reference3 is a REJECTED attempt: preserve its visual style ONLY, its feet are wrong because the same right boot is always lower. FIX this in a new whole-figure drawing, do NOT reproduce those identical stances. The four frames MUST have these exact visible shoe placements: TOP LEFT = right shoe extends DOWN and to the RIGHT below the hem; left shoe lifted close to the hem on the LEFT. TOP RIGHT = passing pose, BOTH shoes close together under the middle of the hem. BOTTOM LEFT = LEFT shoe now extends DOWN and to the LEFT below the hem, and RIGHT shoe is LIFTED close to the hem on the RIGHT. This is the visibly OPPOSITE stance to top-left: the lowest shoe MUST switch sides. BOTTOM RIGHT = passing pose with both shoes close under the hem, subtly different knee bend from top-right. The two extended-stance frames must read as clearly alternating legs even at tiny game size. No fixed right-foot-low pose across frames. Exactly two legs/shoes, no extra feet. All frames same full character, style, scale, raised hands and stable torso/head, do not make the walking by vertically bouncing the same picture. Crisp detailed cozy pixel art, not vector. Keep entire body/bun/hands/shoes well inside each cell with generous transparent margins, no clipping, no pot/tray, no props, no text, no grid, no shadow, no colored rectangle, genuine alpha background.
```
